Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use FinTrack’s manager and member portals, why it is used, and the choices available to you. FinTrack helps managers administer chit schemes, member accounts and payments.

1. Who is responsible for your information

FinTrack is provided as a platform for scheme managers. Your manager decides which member and scheme information to enter and how it is used to administer their schemes. For information held in a manager’s account, contact that manager first. The organization operating your FinTrack deployment is responsible for operating the service and its infrastructure.

2. Information handled

  • Account details: name, username, email address, phone number, profile photo, account role and password credentials. Passwords are stored as password hashes rather than plain text.
  • Member and scheme records: contact details, join date and status, scheme and ticket details, installment schedules, winners, notifications and payment history entered by a manager or generated while using the service.
  • Payment and payout details: bank account holder, account number, IFSC and bank details you choose to save for payouts; payment method, amount, date, transaction reference (UTR) and any payment proof link submitted for verification.
  • Technical and usage information: the session token and account information kept in your browser’s session storage while signed in, plus technical request and security information that may be recorded by the service host or infrastructure providers.

3. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, link members to schemes, show schedules and account details, record and verify payments, process payouts, send account and payment emails, deliver notifications, support password resets, prevent misuse, and maintain and troubleshoot the service.

4. Payments and service providers

When a manager enables online checkout, FinTrack uses Razorpay to create and verify payments. The payment provider handles payment credentials such as card or bank details; FinTrack records payment and transaction details needed to update your account. FinTrack does not ask you to enter card details into its own forms. For manual transfers, your manager may show their payment details, and FinTrack may store the transaction reference or proof link you submit.

FinTrack also relies on the configured hosting and database providers, and may use an email delivery provider for login or password-reset messages. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy notices. The providers and locations used can depend on the deployment configuration.

5. Sharing

Your manager and authorized staff can access the member, scheme and payment information needed to administer their schemes, including payout details where relevant. Information may also be processed by the service providers described above, or disclosed when required by law, to protect users and the service, or to resolve a payment or security issue. FinTrack does not use account information to serve advertising.

6. Storage, security and retention

Account and scheme data is stored in the database configured for the FinTrack deployment. We use safeguards such as password hashing and authenticated access, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and sign out when using a shared device.

Information is kept while an account or scheme needs it to operate, and for any additional period needed for payment records, disputes, legal obligations or service security. Managers should remove or correct member information that is no longer needed. Backups and provider logs may persist for a limited period after deletion.

7. Your choices and requests

You can update some profile and bank details in the member portal. To access, correct or request deletion of member or scheme information, contact your scheme manager. A manager can update records they control. Some payment or account information may need to be retained to complete transactions, keep required records or address disputes. For a service or security concern, contact the administrator of the FinTrack deployment.

8. Children

FinTrack is an account and scheme administration service, not a service directed to children. A manager should only add member information when they have the authority and any permissions required to do so.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above shows when the current version took effect. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to future use of the service.
